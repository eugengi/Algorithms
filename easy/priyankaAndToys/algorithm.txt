Question: Priyanka and Toys
Level: Easy
Author: amititkgp
Solution: Eugene Mwangi & Ndeda Kaduki.

    Problem Statement:
    The shipping company has a requirement that all items loaded in a container must
    weigh less than or equal to 4 units plus the weight of the minimum weight item.
    All items meeting that requirement will be shipped in one container. What is the
    smallest number of containers that can be contracted to ship the items based on
    the given list of weights?

    Algorithm:

        1) Sort the list of items to be shipped.
        2) Pick the minimum weight item out of the list.
        3) Add the number 4 to the minimum weight item.
        4) Increment the container counter for an item weight not within 4 units of the minimum item weight.
        5) Return the number of containers Priyanka must contract to ship all of the toys.
