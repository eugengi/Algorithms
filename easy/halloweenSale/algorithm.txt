Question: Halloween Sale
Level: Easy
Author: kevinsogo
Solution: eugengi

    Problem Statement:
    You wish to buy video games from the famous online video game store Mist.

    Usually, all games are sold at the same price, `p` dollars. However, they 
    are planning to have the seasonal Halloween Sale next month in which you can
    buy games at a cheaper price. Specifically, the first game will cost `p` 
    dollars, and every subsequent game will cost `d` dollars less than the 
    previous one. This continues until the cost becomes less than or equal to
    `m` dollars, after which every game will cost `m` dollars.
    
    How many games can you buy during the Halloween Sale?

    Abbreviations:
    `p`: starting game price
    `d`: discount game price
    `m`: minimum game price
    `s`: customer budget or wallet


    Pseudocode:

    1) Get sequence of all the discount prices, `d` before the minimum
       price, `m`.
    2) Compute sum of the discount prices sequence and check if within customer
       budget `s`.
    3) If computed sum is greater or equals customer budget, `s`, return number
       of games within budget, else proceed.
    4) Compute customer budget balance after purchasing discount price games,
       `d` before minimum price, `m`.
    5) Compute number of games remaining customer balance can purchase at the
       minimum price, `m`.
    6) Sum and return the total games from both discount game prices before `d`
       and at the minimum price, `m` (if any).
